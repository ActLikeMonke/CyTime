# Employee guide

## Sign in and profile

Sign in with your work email and password. Under **Settings → Profile**, keep your name and email current, review your effective-dated weekly schedule, and download a copy of your data as JSON or CSV. Under **Settings → Security**, manage your password and available sign-in factors.

To stop using CyTime, choose **Deactivate account** in Profile and confirm with your password. You will be signed out. Your official time records remain available for the retention period; personal account data is anonymized after that period.

## Record working time

Open **My Time** to clock in and out or enter a completed time period manually. Select a day, week, or month to review your own entries and balances. The calendar is available from **My Time – Calendar**.

Manual forms start with **08:00–16:30** and a **30-minute break**, giving **8 hours** of working time. Use **Add another work period** to enter a split day, then save all periods together. For example, **08:00–11:00** and **13:00–17:00**, each with zero additional break minutes, gives **7 hours** worked. The two-hour gap is automatically counted as a break for daily rule checks and must not be deducted again. Enter only extra breaks taken within each period.

Periods cannot overlap. You can edit or delete individual periods in My Time; the calendar day dialog also lists every period and provides separate edit actions and an option to add more. Clocking out and later clocking in again records another period on the same day. Weekly balances, supervisor summaries, and exports include all periods.

You may not add entries for future dates. Only today's end time can be booked ahead; it remains planned until that time has passed. Rule checks can show a warning when a recorded period conflicts with working-time limits. Warnings do not block saving.

Elapsed planned entries become final through the scheduled task, or when you next open an authenticated page if the scheduler has not run. This also catches up plans from previous days. An open clock-in without an end time still requires you to clock out.

An open clock-in is displayed as **Clocked in** until you clock out. Review the displayed status and net time after saving. Corrections are subject to the application's date and validation rules.

## Understand your balances

**Hours behind this week** compares finalized work with the target for completed workdays before today. Today is not counted as overdue yet.

**Overall balance since first schedule** adds up finalized hours worked minus scheduled target hours from the start of your earliest work schedule through the end of the current week. It uses the schedule valid on each date and reduces targets for NRW holidays, approved vacation, and pending or approved sick days on your workdays. A positive value is overtime; a negative value is below the target, but also includes the remaining target for the current week.

For example, if you have no previous overtime or undertime and a 40-hour week, finishing an 8-hour Monday gives an overall balance of **-32 hours** because Tuesday through Friday are still included in the target. It does not mean you have already missed 32 hours. Missing records since a historical schedule start date also make this balance more negative.

## Report an absence

Open **Absences** and choose **Sick day** or **Vacation**, then select the date range.

- Submit a sick-day report promptly. It counts as an absence immediately, including while pending. You may indicate whether a sick note was submitted.
- A vacation request remains pending until a supervisor approves it. Pending vacation does not reduce the weekly target; approved vacation does.
- Check your annual entitlement, carry-over, used days, pending days, and remaining leave on the Absences page. The displayed vacation-day estimate uses scheduled workdays and NRW public holidays.
- Pending requests can be cancelled from the request list.

Never enter a diagnosis, reason for illness, or medical document. CyTime only records that a day is a sick day and whether a sick note was submitted.
