# Supervisor guide

## Review the team

Open **Supervisor dashboard** to review employee status, leave usage and remaining entitlement, and hour balances. Filter for employees with open requests, change the sort field and direction, or open an employee's detail page to review monthly time entries, absences, and weekly balances.

The dashboard's month selector downloads a CSV of final worked hours for the selected month. The export contains employee IDs and names, so store it only in an approved location.

## Review absence requests

Open **Absence approvals** to process pending requests. Approve or reject each request. You may add an optional comment to a vacation decision; comments are disabled for sick reports to avoid collecting sensitive health information. Sick reports count as absences as soon as they are submitted, regardless of review status.

## Manage employee settings

Open **Employees** to view and manage employee accounts. From an employee detail page, authorized supervisors can update the employee's role, leave entitlement, and effective-dated working schedule. Schedule changes apply from the selected effective date, so historical balances continue to use the schedule that applied at the time.

Role and leave-entitlement changes are audited. The application protects the last active supervisor from being demoted or deactivating their account.

## Privacy and access

Supervisor access is limited to the management functions provided by the application. Do not record medical explanations or documents in comments. Employees can export their own data from **Settings → Profile**; a supervisor's export links, if used, always contain only that supervisor's own data.
